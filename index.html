INTEGRATED DECISION / NORTH STAR

A sequential assessment with controlled reassessment loopback

  1. FOFA: governed federation ranks highest analytically.
  2. MCVSM: C2 − C1 passes collective and required-member viability in the synthetic base case.
  3. FACEM: establishes the authority, commitment, handoff, and evidence architecture.
  4. BACRM: bounds CAP-RANK-001; readiness remains distinct from accountable release.
  5. MCVSM loopback: C3 − C2 quantifies the bounded-AI increment only after the bounded capability case.
  6. Stress: member failure can block progression despite a positive collective result.
Sequential, not automatic. Reassessment can loop backward from operating evidence, AI monitoring, a failed member threshold, or a recovery condition. No displayed result authorizes implementation, substitutes for qualified review, or demonstrates real-world effectiveness.
