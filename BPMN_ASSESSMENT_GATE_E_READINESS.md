# BPMN Assessment Gate E Readiness

**Status:** Candidate implementation; read-only/download-only output

Gate E projects the staged controlled BPMN source and Gates A–D into a qualified dossier with a process-boundary map, risk/control matrix, bounded-AI suitability view, and explicit-assumption efficiency hypotheses.

## Boundary preserved

- The dossier is browser-local, read-only, deterministic, and download-only.
- It preserves unresolved findings rather than treating output availability as a review pass.
- ERIR IDs remain unverified references; the dossier does not determine applicability, effectiveness, compliance, or violations.
- Bounded-AI cases retain Case 0, abstention, manual fallback, and `HUMAN_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED`.
- Hypotheses remain unquantified; no cost, savings, ROI, or effectiveness claim is generated.
- No public writeback, Authority Envelope change, acceptance, commitment, or implementation action occurs.
